Each cell feature the map colours from is predicted from the engine's decoded parameters. Targets are
measured from the real engine's audio (one held note, MIDI 48): the four spectral ratios over the twelve
harmonic magnitudes of the rendered note (the map's own reduction, so on its scale), and energy as the
affine regression of the map's energy channel on the note's RMS. The ratio targets are the measured values themselves (identity).
Held-out = a shuffled 20% of the stances never used to fit or to choose; R2/RMSE/Spearman are of the
prediction against the audio target. 'map' rows give today's map channel as the predictor of the same
target (raw, and after its best affine image), on the same rows.
